FOUNDER & CREATIVE DIRECTOR

ZIZOU DESIGN is an independent creative project exploring photography, atmosphere, color, memory, and visual storytelling.

Each collection is built around a distinct mood and visual language, transforming photographed moments into composed works that extend beyond the original image.

Through photography, graphic composition, interior presentation, and carefully developed collections, ZIZOU DESIGN approaches each piece as both an image and an object of expression.

The work is guided by observation, personal experience, and the belief that ordinary places, colors, light, and moments can carry meaning when they are seen differently.

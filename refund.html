Refund Policy

LAST UPDATED: SEPTEMBER 2026

ALL DIGITAL SALES ARE FINAL

ZIZOU DESIGN sells digital products that are delivered electronically and may be made available for download immediately after purchase.

Because digital products cannot be returned in the same manner as physical goods, all purchases are considered final once the purchased digital product has been delivered, made available for download, or access to the product has otherwise been provided.

ZIZOU DESIGN does not generally provide refunds, returns, or exchanges for digital products after delivery, including when a customer changes their mind, no longer wants the artwork, purchases the wrong artwork, or decides not to use the purchased product.

This policy does not exclude or restrict any consumer right that cannot legally be waived or limited under applicable law.

CORRUPTED, MISSING, OR INACCESSIBLE FILES

If a purchased digital file is genuinely corrupted, incomplete, missing, inaccessible, or cannot be downloaded as intended because of a technical problem, please contact ZIZOU DESIGN with sufficient information to identify the purchase and describe the problem.

ZIZOU DESIGN will first attempt to resolve the issue by restoring access, correcting the problem, or providing a replacement copy of the purchased file where appropriate.

A technical issue does not automatically entitle the customer to a refund if the purchased product can reasonably be repaired, replaced, or made accessible.

If a genuine technical problem cannot reasonably be resolved and prevents the customer from receiving or using the purchased product, ZIZOU DESIGN may consider an appropriate remedy, including a refund where appropriate or required.

DUPLICATE OR INCORRECT CHARGES

If you believe that you were charged more than once for the same transaction or charged an incorrect amount because of a billing or processing error, please report the issue so that the transaction can be reviewed.

A verified duplicate or incorrect charge may be corrected or refunded as appropriate. Because Lemon Squeezy acts as the Merchant of Record for purchases processed through its checkout, any applicable billing correction or refund may be processed through Lemon Squeezy.

NON-REFUNDABLE CIRCUMSTANCES

Except where required by applicable law or otherwise determined by Lemon Squeezy under its Merchant of Record responsibilities, refunds are generally not provided for reasons including:

changing your mind after purchase; purchasing the wrong artwork; deciding that you no longer need or want the product; failing to review the product description before purchasing; dissatisfaction based solely on personal preference; or failing to download or use a product that was properly delivered and made available to you.

Purchases made through the checkout available on the ZIZOU DESIGN website are processed by Lemon Squeezy, which acts as the Merchant of Record and authorized reseller for those transactions.

ZIZOU DESIGN establishes this Refund Policy for its digital products. Transactions processed through Lemon Squeezy are also subject to Lemon Squeezy's applicable buyer terms, refund procedures, and Merchant of Record responsibilities.

Lemon Squeezy may process or issue a refund in accordance with its own terms, policies, legal obligations, and Merchant of Record responsibilities. REFUND REQUESTS

Submitting a refund request does not create an automatic right to a refund.

If you believe that your purchase involves a genuine technical problem, duplicate charge, billing error, or another circumstance that warrants review, please provide the relevant order information and a description of the issue.

ZIZOU DESIGN may review the circumstances and attempt to resolve the underlying problem before considering a refund.

Any refund involving a transaction processed through Lemon Squeezy remains subject to Lemon Squeezy's applicable terms, procedures, and Merchant of Record responsibilities.

If a refund is approved or otherwise issued for a transaction processed through Lemon Squeezy, the refund will be processed through Lemon Squeezy and the applicable payment system.

The time required for refunded funds to appear may depend on the original payment method, Lemon Squeezy, the customer's bank or financial institution, and other payment-processing factors.

CHARGEBACKS & PAYMENT DISPUTES

Customers experiencing a legitimate problem with a purchase are encouraged to report the issue before initiating a chargeback so that the problem can be investigated and an appropriate resolution can be attempted.

Nothing in this section restricts any payment dispute or consumer right that cannot lawfully be restricted.

Lemon Squeezy may independently take action regarding suspected fraud, refund abuse, chargebacks, or payment disputes in accordance with its own terms and Merchant of Record responsibilities.

CONSUMER RIGHTS

Nothing in this Refund Policy is intended to exclude, waive, or limit rights or remedies that cannot legally be excluded, waived, or limited under applicable consumer protection law.

If applicable law requires a refund, cancellation, withdrawal right, or other remedy despite this final-sale policy, the applicable legal requirement will take precedence.
